Research is easier to build on when the reasoning is written down alongside the result. This notebook is a place to keep that context: the question, the experiment, the evidence, and what remains uncertain.

## Keeping the context

A finished figure rarely tells the whole story. Choices about baselines, evaluation, and implementation all shape how a result should be read. Notes here can make those choices explicit.

## Making results useful

A useful research note should make it possible to understand what was tested and how to follow up:

- The question and the hypothesis.
- The setup, evidence, and limitations.
- Links to code, data, and related work.

Small findings, failed experiments, and open questions belong here too. Writing them down makes the next experiment easier to choose.

## Following the questions

The notebook will grow as new experiments are ready to share. Each entry can stand on its own, with enough context to return to it later.
